About the project

Turning print data into smarter decisions

PrintGuard AI explores how machine learning can support 3D printing by learning patterns from previously labelled print-job data.

The problem we are addressing

3D prints can fail because of unsuitable settings, material conditions, temperature variation, bed adhesion issues and other process factors. Detecting risk early can reduce wasted material, time and repeated trials.

Our approach: use selected print parameters as model inputs and predict a likely outcome or failure category based on the training data supplied to the model.
